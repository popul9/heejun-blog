I tought this song was intentionally released feeling unfinished, relatively rushed compared to his other albums, in line with the overall intent of the album, which felt like a manifesto saying *I'm done with this!!*, shaped by the pressures Prince was under at the time and the context of the era.

<br/>

Because he deliberately put it out rough and unpolished, wanting to pop the balloon of his overwhelming public image as a "superstar" as quickly as possible.
So I thought this was a genuinely great song, but one that made me feel a pang of regret, thinking it would have been incredible if he had refined it a little more. (Though I loved even the Prince that made me feel that regret.)

<br/>

This is the song that brought me full circle, making me rethink the entire intent behind his album. I can't stop thinking about what was really going on behind the scenes of its making.
