Is this an occupational hazard of being a developer, or is it just me?

Sometimes, building software feels like playing The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.

I'll be driving and a thought pops up. Wait.. could I build this? Then I'm talking it through with AI, figuring out what I need to do, and giving it a go. Once I understand the basic principle, the implementation is a piece of cake.
Oh, if this works, doesn't that mean this other thing could work too? And off I go again, trying that like a maniac.

After getting completely carried away building things, I pause. Wait, okay, I can build this. But is it worth building? Have I made it way too complicated?
Have I just made something that caters to my own very specific tastes, taken to the extreme?

Then I come across some syntax I've never seen before. Oh, that's interesting. You can write code like this in .NET?
Hmm.. but this makes the code less simple. Does it offer any clear advantage over the existing syntax?

Another ordinary day of getting lost in the work, and suddenly: is this really worth anything? Sure, enjoying it should be enough, but can the joy of technology alone keep me going? Does the effort I'm putting in make financial sense in the market? Does that make me a developer who specialises in chasing things that don't pay off?

I dig into UX and start thinking, at this point, should I just make a game? I want users to feel free to control things, but I shouldn't throw so much at them that it gets overwhelming. Apparently I can develop in C# with Unity on a Mac too. Should I give it a try? Into the TODO notes it goes.

Now AI is getting into philosophy too? They're not just training it on the "right answers" anymore, but taking the logical "flow" of my own thoughts and training it on that separately?? Should I play it safe and learn to bake bread in advance? But isn't baking just a matter of time too? Won't machines eventually take over that as well?

Hmm..
I think I've wandered pretty deep into the forest of IT. There are far too many things besides games to get lost in.
