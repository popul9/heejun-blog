## Weekends are for cafes

Idle Hands. A friend from work said this was their favourite coffee spot, so we went.

Scrolling through Google Maps, I spotted what looked like a cinnamon bun. A knot, to be precise—that twisted shape. We'd had an absolutely magical cinnamon bun experience in Melbourne, so that was pretty much an automatic “we're going.”

![The blue menu board at Idle Hands](./menu.webp)
The menu.

![Cardamom buns and other baked goods on display](./pastries.webp)

Plot twist: it wasn't a cinnamon bun. It was a cardamom bun 🤣.

My experience with cardamom is… biting into it at a Sri Lankan restaurant and being absolutely floored by the aroma. But hey, gave it a go anyway.

![A cardamom bun and chocolate cookie on a plate](./cardamom-bun-and-cookie.webp)

Looks like this.
Seemed pretty small before ordering, but once it's in your hands, it's bigger than you'd think.

![Holding a knot-shaped cardamom bun](./cardamom-bun.webp)

Yep, that's cardamom… packs a serious punch. Still warm too, which was nice.

Figured we'd still be hungry if that was all we had for breakfast, so we got a baguette sandwich too.
Apple / brie / blueberry.

![A baguette with apple, brie and blueberry jam](./apple-brie-baguette.webp)

Fresh bread. Simple, clean flavours. Delicious.

![A baguette sandwich with a pointy end](./baguette-shape.webp)

Funny shape too.

There's a furniture shop out the back of the cafe as well. Fewer seats than we'd expected, so we joined some other people at the end of a table.
