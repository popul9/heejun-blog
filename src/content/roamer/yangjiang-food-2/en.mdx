> This is part of my [two weeks in Guangdong, China](/roamer/china-2026/) travel series.

[In part one, I wrote about cheung fun, rice noodles, goose and dim sum](/roamer/yangjiang-food/). There was still plenty more food from Yangjiang to talk about, so I decided to carry on with part two.

## Another dinner

![A soup with leafy greens and century egg](./century-egg-vegetable-soup.avif)

This soup was made with century egg. It was not a flavour I was used to, but the aroma of the cured egg gave it something quite distinctive.

![Goose offal soup with several different cuts](./goose-offal-soup.avif)

I think this was a goose offal soup. It had all sorts of different parts in it.

![A clear soup with pork and bitter melon](./bitter-melon-pork-soup.avif)

![A small bowl of pork and bitter melon soup](./bitter-melon-pork-soup-close-up.avif)

What stuck with me was the pork and the bitter melon. The melon really did live up to its name. The broth was wonderfully clean and refreshing, but the bitter melon tasted almost like bitter medicine. haha. Apparently it is very good for you, though.

![A cooked goose head on a plate](./goose-head.avif)

I ate goose head too! They say the cheek is the best part. I also tried the brain. It was very soft and mushy, and I could not pick out much of a flavour. What felt strange was the thought that I was eating another living creature's brain. That thought unsettled me more deeply than I expected. It also made me question the habit of picking and choosing only the parts I like when another life has been taken to feed me. It was quite an experience..haha. I ate what I was given with gratitude.

![Fish soup made with medicinal herbs](./fish-herbal-soup.avif)

This soup had fish, medicinal herbs and a few other ingredients. The broth was quite bitter for my taste.

![Crisp pieces of fried fish](./fried-fish.avif)

Fried fish. I dipped it in chilli sauce and happily ate the lot.

![Thin slices of chewy beef with shredded ginger](./sliced-beef-with-ginger.avif)

Thin, chewy slices of beef with shredded ginger. It felt like the sort of thing you would have with a drink. The beef was incredibly chewy in a good way. Delicious.

## Dinner with my wife's relatives

![Dinner around a large table with my wife's relatives](./family-dinner-room.avif)

We had dinner with my wife's relatives!

![A clear chicken soup](./chicken-soup.avif)

This reminded me a little of samgyetang. The chicken was incredibly tender and the broth tasted beautifully clean.

This was my favourite food of the whole trip. The tofu is dark inside because it is made with black sesame. It melts in your mouth, and you get the nuttiness of the sesame together with the flavour of tofu. I polished it off in no time.

![Fried fish with a stir fry of vegetables and nuts](./fried-fish-and-vegetable-stir-fry.avif)

Fried fish, plus vegetables stir fried with nuts.

![Roast duck with crisp skin and everything needed to wrap it](./roast-duck.avif)

![Wrapping roast duck and vegetables in a thin pancake](./roast-duck-wrap.avif)

This one felt more like a dish from northern China. We wrapped the duck, crispy skin and all, in thin pancakes with the other ingredients. haha.

## Dim sum

![Steamed noodles topped with greens and beef tendon](./steamed-beef-tendon-noodles.avif)

![A chewy piece of beef tendon served with the noodles](./beef-tendon-close-up.avif)

Judging from the photos, the chewy pieces look like beef tendon. They were served over steamed noodles with greens and meat, and the whole thing was seriously good.

![Chicken feet chopped through the bone and served with boiled peanuts](./chopped-chicken-feet.avif)

![A close look at a piece of chicken foot chopped through the bone](./chopped-chicken-feet-close-up.avif)

The chicken feet were unusual because they had been chopped straight through the bone. They tasted as though the chicken had been prepared that very morning.

![An assortment of steamed buns and translucent dumplings](./assorted-dim-sum.avif)

![The filling inside a steamed bun](./filled-steamed-bun.avif)

All sorts of unusual dim sum. In Guangdong, every restaurant seems to have its own shapes and flavours. I was told that competition between the restaurants has encouraged them to keep coming up with more variety.

![A chewy black cake with a gentle herbal aroma](./black-herbal-cake.avif)

![A piece of the chewy black cake held with chopsticks](./black-herbal-cake-close-up.avif)

A black... cake of some kind? I do not think it was made from rice. It was very chewy and had a gentle medicinal herb aroma. I liked it.
