## Well, that's different

![Soup with vegetables and century egg](./century-egg-vegetable-soup.avif)

Preserved egg + soup. An unusual aroma. I reckon after two or three tries I'd get used to it and start craving it.

![Goose offal soup with several different parts](./goose-offal-soup.avif)

All sorts of bits of goose in there. Oh, a head too.

![A goose head on a plate](./goose-head.avif)

So I ate goose head! The cheek is the best bit. Tried the brain too. Very mushy, not much flavour.

It felt as though I was eating another creature's consciousness. Mentally, that was a very new experience.



![Clear soup with pork and bitter melon](./bitter-melon-pork-soup.avif)

![A small bowl of broth with pork and bitter melon](./bitter-melon-pork-soup-close-up.avif)

Pork and bitter melon. The melon really is bitter. The broth is so refreshing, but the melon tastes like the bitter medicine I had as a kid. Apparently it's very good for you.

![Soup with fish and medicinal herbs](./fish-herbal-soup.avif)

Soup with fish and medicinal herbs. With that strong herbal medicine flavour, it feels like it has to be doing you good.

![Crisply fried fish](./fried-fish.avif)

Fried fish. Ate it happily with chilli sauce.

![Thinly sliced beef mixed with shredded ginger](./sliced-beef-with-ginger.avif)

Thin, chewy beef with sliced ginger. So chewy, I bet it has plenty of devoted fans. I liked it too.

## Dim sum, once more
My wife's uncle, who used to be in the military, invited us out the next morning, so we went to a famous dim sum place. 

![Noodles topped with vegetables and meat](./steamed-beef-tendon-noodles.avif)

![A chewy piece of meat served with the noodles](./beef-tendon-close-up.avif)

Some incredibly chewy cut with noodles that seemed to have been steamed. So good. Tendon, maybe? 

![Chicken feet chopped through the bone, served with boiled peanuts](./chopped-chicken-feet.avif)

![A piece of chicken foot chopped through the bone](./chopped-chicken-feet-close-up.avif)

Chicken feet chopped through the bone. You know how in comics they sometimes make it look like you can feel a living chicken in your mouth? This really tasted fresh enough to have been prepared from a chicken slaughtered that morning. 

![Assorted dim sum, including steamed buns and dumplings with translucent wrappers](./assorted-dim-sum.avif)

![A steamed bun split open to show the filling](./filled-steamed-bun.avif)

All sorts of dim sum. Every restaurant brings out its own shapes and flavours like this. Each one trying something different as they compete.

![A black cake with a subtle herbal aroma](./black-herbal-cake.avif)

![A chewy black cake held in chopsticks](./black-herbal-cake-close-up.avif)

A black.. cake? Doesn't seem to be made from rice, but it's really chewy with a gentle herbal aroma. Nice.

![Grilled fish served on hot stones](./grilled-fish-on-hot-stones.avif)

![Golden dim sum rolls](./fried-dim-sum-rolls.avif)

![A table of dim sum and fried noodles](./assorted-dim-sum-table.avif)

![Yellow siu mai in a bamboo steamer](./siu-mai.avif)
Actually, this is yet another dim sum place. Went to so many that I'm putting them all here. Yep.
