## The fast train

The journey takes about two hours. Looking out at the scenery, you really get a feel for how warm Guangzhou is and how much water there is. With a big port here too (apparently this was where most of the tea was traded), no wonder the region seems to have been so busy and wealthy for so long.

![Rainy green countryside in Guangdong through the train window](./guangdong-countryside-from-train.avif)

![Yangjiang station and its surroundings under a clear sky](./yangjiang-railway-station.avif)

And here we are in Yangjiang, with clear blue skies!
