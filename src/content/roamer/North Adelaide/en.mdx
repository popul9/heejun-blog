## Coffee first
![blog placeholder](./espresso_cup.webp)
Coffee first! A long black. Tasty. Maybe because it's Italian style, it's strong.


![blog placeholder](./vintage_italian_posters.webp)
![blog placeholder](./mercato_cafe_seating.webp)
While taking in the vintage Italian posters and the mood around..

![blog placeholder](./cornetto_sfogliatella_plates.webp)
![blog placeholder](./sfogliatella_plate.webp)
the pastries come out. yummmmm. Ordered a chocolate lobster tail + vanilla cornetto.
First time having a cornetto, and I'm not sure I can tell how it tastes different from a croissant.
The lobster tail is seriously seriously crispy. That softness made out of all those thin layers.




![blog placeholder](./poached_eggs_bacon_toast.webp)
The savoury one came out too. Nothing too special given what the menu is, but having cafe bacon after a while feels good.


![blog placeholder](./mercato_receipt.webp)
receipt. No weekend surcharge and the price is satisfying.
Hope this quality keeps up. The staff were cheerful and the vibe was good. Thank you ☺️.
