## Why?

My wife is from Yangjiang in Guangdong, so we went to China to see her family + get some rest.
This was less a trip where we rushed around ticking off famous sights and more a couple of weeks spent eating, shopping and simply living alongside people. So rather than arranging everything day by day, I want to write about the small pieces of everyday life we actually experienced.
Guangdong is famous for food, especially its distinctive yum cha culture, so my expectations were very high. :)

- Yangjiang, Guangdong: the city at the centre of this trip
- Ganzhou, Jiangxi: a short three-day trip away from Yangjiang

The high-speed train from Guangzhou to Yangjiang takes about two hours. Yangjiang is a coastal city with a modest population of, well, around three million. It is famous in China as the city of knives and scissors; well-known knife brands such as Shibazi (十八子作) began there too.
