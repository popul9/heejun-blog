A while ago I posted ['UI is Hard'](/en/developer/ui-is-hard/), and a friend saw it and showed me this.

![The main screen of Worst UI Challenge and its volume control UI entries](./worst-ui-challenge.avif)

It's a [challenge](https://worst-ui-challenge.vercel.app/) where you build the worst possible volume control UI, apparently.

Apparently there's a separate [Reddit channel](https://www.reddit.com/r/badUIbattles/) too.

This is fun? I had only ever thought about complaining that UI is hard. Deliberately wrecking one (at least lately) never crossed my mind.

Hmm.. Let me take this brain of mine, which always charged straight ahead chasing nothing but efficiency, and run it as inefficiently as possible, like a fish swimming backward, to build an inefficient UI.

So this post is the paradox of my previous post, and also an homage, a tribute to Richard P. Gabriel's [Worse is Better is Worse](https://dreamsongs.com/Files/worse-is-worse.pdf): **UI is Hard is Easy**. Haha 😆.


## Core Idea

![A footy and concentric target rings, with volume 100% shown in the center](./rugby-volume-target.avif)

Kick the footy and land it inside the circle. The closer you get to the target, the closer the volume gets to 100.

I handed it off to Astra (Codex), and it took about thirty minutes and a few prompts.
Drag the ball and let go, and it kicks. Good luck hitting it.. haha.

Today's topic is wrecking UX? Sure, but my UX values still apply. I make sure the user's actions stay in control.
Put another way: there may be a lot you can't control, but I'll give you just one thing you can control.

## Inspiration

The inspiration came from **Pro Jank Footy**, which I saw at a game expo earlier this year. I shared it [here](/en/roamer/sage-2026/).

![Pro Jank Footy's Steam page and gameplay](./pro-jank-footy.avif)
The ball moved so strangely that I asked the developer how they made it. Under the hood it's a 3D engine, but what players see is a 2D filter laid on top. That stuck in my head for a while and I kept wondering when I'd get to use it. Well, here we are..lol

The game recently had its full release on Steam. When I took that screenshot the reviews looked pretty good? It doesn't support Mac, so I haven't bought it yet.

I also read in an article that a Korean artist living in Adelaide, [hyemina](https://hyemina.com/about), worked on it too, and I was so happy to see that. (I'm Korean too)

![Distinctive work from hyemina studio](./hyemina-studio.avif)

She seems to do a lot of interesting work. It's full of personality and cute.


![Codex usage and the available reset indicator](./codex-usage.avif)
Anyway, this project used up my token quota for today.
Codex resets my limits way too generously. I love it. I do not love Astra's token burn rate.
