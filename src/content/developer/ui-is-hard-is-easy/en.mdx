A while ago I posted ['UI is Hard'](/en/developer/ui-is-hard/), and a friend saw it and showed me these.

- [r/badUIbattles](https://www.reddit.com/r/badUIbattles/)
- [Worst UI Challenge](https://worst-ui-challenge.vercel.app/)

Ha, this is fun. I had only ever thought about complaining that UI is hard. Deliberately wrecking one (at least lately) never crossed my mind.

Hmm.. Let me take this brain of mine, which always swims straight ahead like a fish chasing efficiency, and make it swim as slowly as possible. Time to build an inefficient UI.

So this post is the paradox of my previous post, and also my homage to Richard P. Gabriel's [Worse is Better is Worse](https://dreamsongs.com/Files/worse-is-worse.pdf): **UI is Hard is Easy**. Haha 😆.

Okay, here's my idea.

Kick the rugby ball and land it inside the circle. The closer you get to the target, the closer the volume gets to 100.

I handed it off to Astra (Codex), and it took about thirty minutes and a few prompts. Drag the ball to aim, then let go to kick. Good luck hitting it.. haha.

Just so you know, this doesn't change your device volume. You can hear the result with the Test sound button inside the game.

Today's topic is wrecking UI? Sure, but my UX values still apply. Give the user as much control as possible over what they're operating. Don't pile on cognitive overhead all at once. Worry about one thing at a time.

Put another way: there's a lot you can't control, and it's all complicated, but I'll give you just one thing you can control.

## Inspiration

The inspiration came from **Pro Jank Footy**, which I saw at a game expo earlier this year. I shared it [here](/en/roamer/sage-2026/).

The ball moved so strangely that I asked the developer how they made it. Under the hood it's a 3D engine, but what players see is a 2D filter laid on top. That stuck in my head for a while and I kept wondering when I'd get to use it. Well, here we are..lol

![Pro Jank Footy's Steam page and gameplay](./pro-jank-footy.avif)

The game recently had its full release on Steam. When I took that screenshot the reviews looked pretty good? It doesn't support Mac, so I haven't bought it yet.

I also read in an article that a Korean artist living in Adelaide, [hyemina](https://hyemina.com/about), worked on it too, and I was so happy to see that. (I'm Korean too)
