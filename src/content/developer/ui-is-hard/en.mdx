**UI is hard.**

**I was doomscrolling on Facebook as usual when I came across a video about UI.**

[https://www.facebook.com/reel/2200209323859340](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2200209323859340)

Ooh, pretty cool.

<Video src="/videos/cover-flow-ui-inspiration.mp4" />

So I thought I might try it in the house stories feature I’m adding. It’s a sort of community board where tenants can send each other little updates and things.

I’ve always liked iOS’s Cover Flow, and with skeuomorphism back in the picture, I figured I’d try building it just for fun.

![The Cover Flow UI I tried in House Stories](./house-stories-cover-flow.avif)

<Video src="/videos/house-stories-cover-flow.mp4" />

But, well… it’s not that great.
It’s not terrible, but for starters, it’s slow on an iPhone 13 Pro Max. I could try optimising it, but this kind of thing seems useful for quickly flicking through images, like Cover Flow. Putting text and other things you need to read carefully in it? Doesn’t seem like a great fit.

There are loads of fun animations you can interact with using a mouse cursor. On mobile, though, scrolling up and down is usually pretty much the only way to interact with a UI component, so it’s tricky.

I feel a bit guilty as a developer committing the sin(?) of using something just because the technology makes it possible. But today, it’s fine. Because it’s Sunday.

To wrap up, let me show off Cover Flow on my beloved iPhone 4s running iOS 5.0.1. It gave me an excuse to take it out of the drawer after ages. A productive weekend, I guess?
Even with a mountain of features waiting to ship, weekends are the only time I get to do this sort of thing 😀.
